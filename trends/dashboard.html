🗓️This week's kit

Make these once a week in Gemini (about an hour) and upload each one. They go out on their own over the week.

Outfit images

Trending looks of the week. For each: Copy image prompt, paste it in Gemini (nothing to attach), then Upload the image. No text goes on it: clean look-plus-pieces images are what get saved.

Reels

Same idea: Copy video prompt, in Gemini choose Video and paste, then Upload. We add the look's name, music and the “Comment LINK” ending.

✨Create from a trend

Spotted something trending (korean pants, say)? Make pins, a reel and an Instagram story for it. The link, the Pinterest board and the design are picked for you.

The trend
Where to buy

Paste a product or deal link and we add your tag. Leave it empty to link to the store's search for the trend.

Photos (optional)

Pressing Create opens a ready-filled GitHub page in a new tab. Drag up to 6 photos into its text box there (Gemini images or your own photos, not Amazon's product photos, which Amazon doesn't allow), then press the green Create / Submit new issue button.

3 or more photos make a reel. With none, you get text-only designs and a Gemini prompt for the video.
