Privacy

Last updated 6 November 2026

Get Your Deal shows style ideas trending in India with products to shop. You can browse it without an account, and we don't ask you for any personal information.

What we collect

Nothing directly. The site has no sign-up, no forms, no analytics and no advertising trackers, and it doesn't set cookies of its own.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address to keep the service secure. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

When you go to a store

Buy and "See all" buttons take you to the store (Myntra, AJIO or Amazon), sometimes through a tracking link from an affiliate network. From then on, the store's own privacy policy and cookies apply. We never see what you buy, your payment details or your address.

Children

The site isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect anyone's personal information.

Contact

Questions about this page: Carrercurve@gmail.com. If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
