Get Your Deal

Privacy

Last updated 31 October 2026

Get Your Deal is a list of deals from Indian online stores. You can browse it without an account, and we don't ask you for any personal information.

What we collect

Nothing directly. The site has no sign-up, no forms, no analytics and no advertising trackers, and it doesn't set cookies of its own.

Saved deals

When you tap the heart on a deal, it's saved in your own browser (local storage), not sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address to keep the service secure. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

When you go to a store

Buy buttons take you to the store (for example Amazon, Myntra or AJIO), sometimes through a tracking link from an affiliate network. The link tells the store the visit came from our site. From then on, the store's own privacy policy and cookies apply. We never see what you buy, your payment details or your address.

Sharing

The Share button opens WhatsApp with the deal's link. Nothing is sent unless you send the message yourself.

Children

The site isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect anyone's personal information.
